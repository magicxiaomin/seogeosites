# Pilot Site Direction Selection Report

Status: Draft recommendation for first real SEO/GEO pilot site

## Recommendation

Start with **AI workflow templates for small businesses** as the first real pilot direction.

Why this is the strongest first pilot:

- Low-to-medium risk compared with legal/medical/financial topics.
- Naturally supports SEO pages, templates, checklists, FAQs, and examples.
- Naturally supports GEO extraction because answer engines can reuse direct answers, steps, and decision criteria.
- Evidence can be grounded in official product docs plus original editorial templates.
- Monetization can stay optional until trust and quality are established.

## Guardrails

- Keep all generated pages in staging until source URLs and human review are complete.
- Avoid making productivity-gain guarantees.
- Avoid unsupported tool comparisons.
- Do not add affiliate monetization until disclosure and review rules are in place.
- Prefer original templates/checklists over generic listicles.
